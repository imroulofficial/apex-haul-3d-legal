1. Information we collect

We only collect what the Game needs to run, save your progress, show ads and process purchases.

TypeWhat it isWhy
Anonymous account ID A random identifier created by Firebase Anonymous Authentication. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number. Identify your save data
Game progress Trucks owned, upgrades, in-game currency balances, completed contracts, settings and similar gameplay data stored in Cloud Firestore. Save and restore progress

4. Advertising & consent

The Game is free to play and supported by ads served through Google AdMob:

  • Rewarded ads are always optional. You choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward, such as doubled contract payouts, an emergency refuel or a tow and repair.
  • Interstitial ads are full-screen ads shown at natural breaks, such as returning to the garage after a contract. They are not shown during the opening tutorial missions and are rate-limited.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's settings. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android device settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads (wording may vary by device).
